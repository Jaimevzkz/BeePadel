BeePadel is an open source Pádel tracker app, with availability in WearOS devices. It lets you keep the score of your matches in real time, taking away the part boring part of remembering your score. Your match history can easily be imported or exported to the app using a JSON file. It is also possible to connect the app to Strava, so that your acitivities are synced automatically with it, this last feature is totally optional and turned off by default. The aim for this app it to do one thing, and do it well. Enjoy and don't hesitate to send feedback.
